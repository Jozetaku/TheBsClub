# High-Contrast Official Logo Design

## Goal

Create a new high-contrast version of The B's Club official mountain logo that remains clear on white and warm paper-white backgrounds while preserving the existing brand identity.

## Source

- Use only `campaign-2026-08/assets/brand/logo-official.png` as the visual source.
- Preserve the mountain silhouette, proportions, wordmark spelling, apostrophe, and overall arrangement.
- Do not substitute the simplified cup/mountain logo.

## Colour treatment

- Wordmark: deep brand green `#123F35`.
- Mountain shadows: dark espresso brown `#4A3428`.
- Mountain highlights: retain restrained warm gold and cream accents.
- Background: fully transparent.
- Maintain sufficient contrast for placement on pure white `#FFFFFF` and paper white `#FFFDF8`.

## Deliverables

- High-resolution transparent PNG master.
- White-background preview for visual verification.
- Stable campaign path under `campaign-2026-08/assets/brand/`.
- Do not overwrite the current official logo; create a separate high-contrast version.

## Quality checks

- Compare the generated file against the source at full size.
- Reject altered lettering, missing apostrophe, distorted mountain geometry, halos, opaque background, or low-contrast cream text.
- Confirm it remains readable at both website-header size and A4-header size.
