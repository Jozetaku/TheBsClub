# Red Curry Chicken Bowl Scale Design

## Goal

Create the next approved menu master for Red Curry Chicken from the current website photograph while matching the portion served at The B's Club.

## Source

Use `images/campaign/v6/red-curry-white-ceramic.jpg` as the edit target.

## Image edit

- Reduce only the curry bowl and the serving contained inside it by approximately 20 percent.
- Keep the rice bowl at its current size and position.
- Preserve the red coconut curry, sliced chicken, short-cut long beans, sweet basil, and red chilli exactly as the visible ingredients.
- Reconstruct the exposed wooden serving board naturally where the larger bowl previously appeared.
- Preserve the camera angle, white table, café background, lighting, colour balance, and overall composition.
- Do not add text, logos, watermarks, drinks, utensils, people, or additional ingredients.

## Approval and publishing

The edited image is a candidate until the owner approves it. After approval, it can become the menu master and be used to create the standard Master, Square, Landscape, and Portrait asset pack before updating Obsidian and publishing surfaces.

## Acceptance criteria

- The curry bowl appears about 20 percent smaller than in the source.
- The rice bowl and all non-target scene details remain visually unchanged.
- The dish remains recognisably Red Curry Chicken and contains no inaccurate ingredients.
- The full serving remains suitable for centred marketplace crops.
