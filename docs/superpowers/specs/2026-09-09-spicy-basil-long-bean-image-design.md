# Spicy Basil Chicken Long-Bean Image Design

## Goal

Create the approved marketplace master for Spicy Basil Chicken from the current website photograph, accurately reflecting the restaurant's real ingredients and serving presentation.

## Approved visual change

- Remove every sugar snap pea / snow-pea-like green vegetable from the dish.
- Replace those pieces with cut long-bean segments that are approximately 20% smaller than the removed pieces.
- Keep the replacement quantity visually balanced and realistic for the existing serving.
- Preserve the chicken, Thai basil, sliced red chilli, jasmine rice, white ceramic bowls, wooden board, café background, lighting, camera angle and colour treatment.
- Keep the food centred and fully visible in a square marketplace image suitable for Swinch.
- Do not add cucumber, bell pepper, broccoli, onion, new garnish, text, logos, utensils, hands, borders or watermarks.

## Candidate v2 refinement

- Reduce only the foreground food bowl by 20% relative to Candidate v1 so it better represents the real serving size.
- Keep the separate rice bowl at its current size and preserve its position.
- Preserve the square composition and all other approved visual elements without introducing new ingredients.

## Platform workflow
