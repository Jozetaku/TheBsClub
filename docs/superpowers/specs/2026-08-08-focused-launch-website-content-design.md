# The B's Club Focused Launch Website Content Design

**Date:** 2026-08-08
**Status:** Approved direction; awaiting written-spec review
**Launch target:** 15 August 2026
**Commit gate:** Do not commit website changes until the owner reviews the rendered page

## Purpose

Reframe the homepage around one clear launch message: The B's Club is an Asian Café in central Interlaken offering four Thai/Asian café meals alongside its established Bubble Tea, coffee, matcha and takeaway drink range. The page must help customers immediately understand what is available without inventing products, preparation methods or dietary claims.

The campaign system is **One Hero, One Message**. Food and the leading Brown Sugar Milk Tea share one visual story rather than competing promotions.

## Confirmed Business Scope

- New food consists of exactly four dishes:
  1. Spicy Basil
  2. Green Curry
  3. Red Curry
  4. Crispy Chicken Katsu Curry
- Existing drinks and products remain available.
- The four meals use the same portion and price for dine in and takeaway.
- There is no Thai Quick Bowl or smaller Grab & Go food size at launch.
- Grab & Go refers to drinks and existing products, not a second food range.
- Water and Cola are secondary meal-completion items, not hero products or SEO categories.
- Set Menu promotions are future scope. The layout may leave room for them, but no set, inclusion or price may be published now.
- Hours are daily 11:00–21:00.

## Positioning and Bio Contract

Account/page name:

> **The B's Club | Asian Café Interlaken**

Short bio:

> **Asian Café in Interlaken 🏔️**<br>
> Thai Food • Bubble Tea • Coffee & Matcha<br>
> Dine In • Takeaway • Grab & Go<br>
> Daily 11:00–21:00

Do not use `Thai & Asian Fusion Café`; the launch offer is Thai-led and does not yet demonstrate a distinct fusion range.

## Homepage Information Architecture

### 1. Hero: One Hero, One Message

- Eyebrow: `Asian Café · Central Interlaken`
- H1: `Asian Café in Interlaken`
- Supporting line: `Thai food. Bubble tea. One colourful stop.`
- Lead: `Thai café meals for dine in or takeaway, plus Bubble Tea, coffee, matcha and drinks to go — every day from 11:00 to 21:00.`
- Primary CTA: `Explore food + drinks`
- Secondary paths: `Get Directions` and `Order on Uber Eats`
- Service language must distinguish `Dine In · Thai Takeaway · Drinks Grab & Go` so visitors do not infer a smaller Grab & Go meal.

The official mountain logo remains top-left and repeats in the footer. The decorative hero continues to show Mini Makers climbing ladders around an oversized food bowl and Bubble Tea. The Bubble Tea cursor retains the simplified `B` cup mark.

### 2. Four-Dish Food Launch

The four food cards must be the most prominent product content below the hero. Each card includes a product image, origin/category, description, choices and prices.

| Dish | Primary image | Choices and prices |
|---|---|---|
| Spicy Basil | Chicken | Chicken CHF 18.50; Tofu option CHF 15.50 |
| Green Curry | Chicken | Chicken CHF 18.50; Tofu option CHF 17.50 |
| Red Curry | Chicken | Chicken CHF 18.50; Tofu option CHF 17.50 |
| Crispy Chicken Katsu Curry | Chicken | CHF 17.50 |

Food cards use `Café Meal · Dine In or Takeaway`. Do not publish gram weights until the actual served meals are weighed. Do not use Vegetarian, Vegan, plant-based or related claims until ingredients and preparation are verified. `Tofu option` is the only approved dietary wording.

### 3. Honest Preparation Note

Use this exact statement near the food cards:

> **Thai recipes prepared by Thai chefs, heated and served at The B's Club.**

Remove or replace broad food claims including `Made fresh`, `prepared to order`, `freshly cooked to order` and `made fresh in-house`. Drink-specific wording may describe drinks as prepared for the customer only when it cannot be read as a claim about the food.

### 4. Most-Loved Drinks

The drinks section uses the confirmed sales order but never publishes the attributed CHF amounts:

1. Brown Sugar Milk Tea
2. Yummy Strawberry
3. Matcha Latte
4. Mango Tea
5. Biscoff Milk Tea

Brown Sugar Milk Tea is the first beverage hero. The existing full Bubble Tea, Matcha and Coffee menu dialogs remain available. Water and Cola appear only in a compact `Complete your meal — water and cola available` treatment or within the wider cold-drinks menu.

The website also presents a prominent four-product bestseller showcase:

1. Brown Sugar Milk Tea as the large lead feature
2. Yummy Strawberry
3. Matcha Latte
4. Mango Tea

On desktop, Brown Sugar occupies the dominant area and the other three drinks form a supporting grid. On mobile, all four stack with Brown Sugar first. Mini Makers and a ladder may appear in the Brown Sugar feature only; the supporting cards remain product-led so the section stays premium and uncluttered.

This showcase is a discovery surface, not a replacement for the full menus. Preserve all existing menu-opening paths:

- `View Bubble Tea menu` opens the Bubble Tea menu image;
- Brown Sugar Milk Tea, Yummy Strawberry and Mango Tea link to the Bubble Tea menu, while Matcha Latte links to the Matcha menu;
- the Bubble Tea, Matcha and Coffee category cards retain their `View menu` actions;
- the mobile quick-action `Menu` button remains available;
- the menu dialog retains the Bubble Tea, Matcha and Coffee tabs.

### 5. Visit and Evening Intent

The evening panel and Visit section should naturally cover lunch, dinner, dine in and takeaway intent. They should reinforce the central Interlaken location and daily 11:00–21:00 hours without claiming restaurant-style cooking that does not occur on site.

## SEO Content Map

Use these phrases naturally and no more than needed:

| Search intent | Primary placement |
|---|---|
| Asian café Interlaken | Page title, H1, structured data and Visit copy |
| Thai food Interlaken | Food-section introduction |
| Bubble tea Interlaken | Meta description and drinks introduction |
| Thai takeaway Interlaken | Food service line |
| Lunch in Interlaken | Food/evening supporting copy |
| Dinner in Interlaken | Evening panel |
| Café near Interlaken center | Visit copy, phrased naturally as central Interlaken |

The title and description should prioritize clarity:

- Title: `The B's Club | Asian Café Interlaken`
- Meta description: `Thai food, Bubble Tea, coffee and matcha at The B's Club, an Asian café in central Interlaken. Dine in or takeaway daily, 11:00–21:00.`

Structured data remains a `CafeOrCoffeeShop` and `Restaurant`, with Asian and Thai cuisine, Bubble Tea and Coffee, the food anchor as `hasMenu`, and closing time 21:00.

## Visual and Asset Coordination

- Preserve the green, yellow, coral and cream system.
- Preserve the official mountain logo, Mini Makers and ladder-climbing signature.
- Chicken is the primary image for Spicy Basil, Green Curry and Red Curry; Tofu remains text-only as an option.
- Do not independently generate campaign product imagery while the print/social task is producing the shared asset set.
- Until exact approved asset paths and usage mapping arrive, retain the current website illustration treatment as a reversible fallback.
- When final assets arrive, integrate only the supplied mapped files so website, storefront, poster and social executions remain visually consistent.
- Motion stays decorative and must respect reduced-motion settings.
- Previously supplied food and drink hero images remain provisional until the owner approves the revised packaging direction.
- The active food hero is Spicy Basil in white or kraft Grab & Go packaging rather than a green ceramic bowl.
- Drink images must follow the real menu references: a squat, wide clear cup; centered black logo; and product-specific layering.
- Brown Sugar uses vertical brown-sugar streaks and pearls, Matcha uses a white-to-green gradient with pearls, and fruit tea uses translucent colour and visible fruit.
- Keep the product layer independent from the Mini Makers/ladder overlay so approved assets can be replaced without rebuilding the layout.

## Extensibility Without Publishing Future Offers

The food and drink structures should support a future Set Menu card or section without changing the four launch dishes. No Set Menu name, contents, saving, price or launch date appears in the current page.

## Acceptance Tests

Automated and browser checks must verify:

- exactly four new food cards and no invented fifth dish;
- Chicken is the primary presentation for the three dual-choice Thai dishes;
- `Tofu option` appears for Spicy Basil, Green Curry and Red Curry;
- no Vegetarian/Vegan/plant-based claim appears;
- the honest Thai-chef preparation statement appears exactly as approved;
- broad made-fresh/to-order food claims do not appear;
- the four featured bestsellers appear in confirmed rank order, with Biscoff Milk Tea remaining available through the full menu rather than the showcase;
- Brown Sugar Milk Tea is the first and largest drink feature;
- the four-product bestseller showcase contains Brown Sugar Milk Tea, Yummy Strawberry, Matcha Latte and Mango Tea;
- the bestseller showcase does not remove or replace the full Bubble Tea, Matcha and Coffee menu-opening paths;
- Water and Cola are present only as secondary meal-completion items;
- no Quick Bowl or Set Menu is published;
- daily hours remain 11:00–21:00;
- the official logo, Mini Makers, ladder and Bubble Tea cursor remain intact;
- desktop, tablet and mobile layouts have no horizontal overflow;
- menu dialogs, analytics consent and directions tracking continue to work.
