# Mango Tea Master Image Design

**Date:** 2026-09-10

**Status:** Owner-approved design, awaiting spec review

**Product:** Mango Tea

**Brand:** Bublee / The B's Club

## Goal

Create a photorealistic Mango Tea master image that belongs to the same visual system as the approved Brown Sugar Milk Tea master. The result must accurately represent the served product and remain reusable across the website, Swinch, social media and print.

## Approved direction

Use the Brown Sugar Milk Tea master as the composition and proportion reference:

- one authentic clear Bublee cup, centred on a wooden serving tray;
- the same shortened, realistic cup proportions and optical size;
- correctly spelled black **Bublee** logo;
- green heat-sealed film lid;
- warm café lighting, white tabletop and softly blurred The B's Club interior;
- food-only product photography with no added promotional text.

## Drink appearance

- Clear, refreshing Mango Tea with ice.
- Colour starts bright mango-yellow at the top and transitions naturally into warm amber-orange toward the bottom.
- The bottom contains translucent white Lychee Popping Boba.
- The Popping Boba layer uses the same approved fill height and visual quantity as the Brown Sugar Milk Tea master. This becomes the default quantity standard for subsequent drink masters unless the owner explicitly changes it.
- No black tapioca pearls, mango jelly, fruit chunks or dark sediment.

## Composition constraints

- Square master composition with sufficient safe area for square, portrait and landscape derivatives.
- Cup remains fully visible and centred; no cropping of lid or base.
- Natural condensation and believable ice are allowed.
- No straw, dome lid, extra drinks, food, hands, loose ingredients, watermark or overlaid menu name.
- Physical branding on the cup and lid is allowed; generated advertising copy is not.

## Canonical copy

**English name:** Mango Tea

**English description:** Golden mango tea with a bright tropical finish and juicy lychee popping boba.

**German name:** Mango Tea

**German description:** Goldener Mango-Tee mit tropisch-frischem Geschmack und saftigen Lychee-Popping-Boba.

## Asset outputs

- `mango-tea-master.png` — approved square master.
- `mango-tea-square-1200.jpg` — Swinch and marketplace use.
- `mango-tea-portrait-1080x1350.jpg` — social and portrait use.
- `mango-tea-landscape-1200x900.jpg` — landscape and general reuse.
- Website derivative at `images/campaign/v2/mango-tea.png` after owner image approval.

No existing live platform asset is replaced until the owner approves the generated candidate.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The cup, logo, lid, tray, lighting and café background visibly match the Brown Sugar visual family.
2. Drink colour matches the owner reference: mango-yellow above and amber-orange below.
3. Only translucent white Lychee Popping Boba appears at the bottom.
4. Popping Boba quantity matches the Brown Sugar master standard.
5. The cup is realistic, centred and fully visible in all intended crops.
6. The word **Bublee** is spelled correctly wherever the physical brand mark appears.
7. The image contains no added text, straw, fruit pieces, extra products or watermark.
8. Website, Swinch and Obsidian updates occur only after explicit owner approval of the candidate.

## Verification

- Visually compare the candidate beside the approved Brown Sugar Milk Tea master.
- Inspect the logo spelling and the Lychee Popping Boba colour at full resolution.
- Preview square, portrait and landscape crops before promotion.
- After promotion, verify file dimensions, hashes, website rendering, Swinch state and Obsidian references.
