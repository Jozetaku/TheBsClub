# Bublee Brown Sugar Milk Tea Image Design

## Goal

Create the first approved master image for the refreshed Bublee drinks collection. Brown Sugar Milk Tea establishes the reusable visual standard for later drink images and must look consistent with the café's food photography.

## Approved visual direction

- Feature one real-looking Bublee takeaway cup as the only product.
- Preserve the proven cup system from the owner references: clear straight-sided cup, green sealed film lid, centered black **Bublee** logo, creamy tan milk tea, natural brown-sugar marbling, and black tapioca pearls settled at the bottom.
- Place the cup upright and centered on the café's white tabletop or wooden serving tray.
- Use warm natural café light and a softly blurred, recognizably authentic café background.
- Keep the full cup visible with comfortable space around it so square, 4:5 portrait, and 4:3 landscape derivatives can be made without cutting the lid, logo, or pearls.

## Accuracy constraints

- Brand spelling is exactly **Bublee**. Do not use Babblee, Bubblee, The B's Club, or invented lettering.
- Keep the green sealed lid and the black printed cup branding consistent with the supplied reference photographs.
- Do not add a straw, dome lid, whipped cream, foam cap, loose pearls on top, food, extra drinks, hands, decorative ingredients, promotional text, or watermarks.
- The drink must remain recognizably Brown Sugar Milk Tea: creamy beige milk tea, visible brown-sugar streaks, ice, and black tapioca pearls.

## Reusable deliverables after image approval

- Immutable high-resolution master.
- 1200 x 1200 marketplace square.
- 1080 x 1350 social portrait.
- 1200 x 900 website landscape.
- Website-specific optimized asset derived from the approved master.

File names use `brown-sugar-milk-tea` and contain no text overlay.

## Copy direction

**English name:** Brown Sugar Milk Tea

**English description:** Creamy milk tea swirled with rich brown sugar and finished with chewy tapioca pearls.

**German name:** Brown Sugar Milk Tea

**German description:** Cremiger Milchtee mit kräftigem Braunzucker und bissfesten Tapiokaperlen.

Copy stays clear and factual. Ingredient and allergen claims remain provisional until the recipe and packaging are owner-verified.

## Approval and publication flow

1. Generate one candidate using the two owner-supplied photographs as visual references.
2. Check cup shape, green seal, logo spelling, drink colour, sugar marbling, pearl placement, and crop safety.
3. Show the candidate to the owner and revise only the requested details.
4. After explicit approval, create the reusable image pack and Brown Sugar Milk Tea Obsidian product record.
5. Update the website and other menu platforms only when the owner asks for publication.

## Acceptance criteria

- The image looks like the drink served by Bublee, not a generic bubble tea stock photo.
- The logo reads **Bublee** and remains unobstructed.
- The cup is the clear focal point and stays fully visible across the planned crops.
- No unserved garnish, topping, prop, or food is introduced.
- The final image and descriptions can be reused consistently across the website, Swinch, Uber Eats, social media, and Obsidian.
