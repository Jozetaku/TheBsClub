Description

Enjoy our freshly prepared Ham, Egg & Cheese Sandwich with one Boba drink of your choice. Pair it with your favourite Boba drink, whether you prefer milk tea, fruit tea, matcha, or another refreshing bubble drink.

Ingredients

Sandwich: Bread, ham, egg, cheese, tomato, cucumber, lettuce, mayonnaise.
Drink: Boba drink of choice (ingredients vary by flavour).

Allergens

The sandwich contains gluten, egg and milk. Mayonnaise may contain mustard, depending on the product used. Boba drink allergens vary depending on the selected flavour and may contain milk, soy or other allergens. If you have any food allergy or intolerance, please contact us before ordering.

Serving Recommendation

Best enjoyed fresh. Shake or stir your Boba drink before drinking. For the best taste and texture, enjoy soon after delivery.
